Climate change

Rising greenhouse gas concentrations are driving global temperatures upward, reshaping weather, sea levels, and ecosystems.

⚖️

Poverty & inequality

Extreme poverty has fallen sharply over decades, but progress is uneven and gains have slowed — while income and wealth remain highly concentrated.

🩺

Public health

Global life expectancy and child survival have improved enormously, yet large gaps persist between regions and disease burden is shifting.

🦋

Biodiversity loss

Wildlife populations and natural habitats are shrinking at an accelerating rate, threatening the ecosystems that sustain life on Earth.

All figures are drawn from public research organizations — Our World in Data, the World Bank, WHO, NASA/NOAA, IUCN, and WWF — and cited beneath each chart. This is an independent, non-commercial project for public awareness.
